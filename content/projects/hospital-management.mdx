## Context

A hospital needed one system for the work around its patients: registration, appointments, medical examinations and their results, doctors and clinics, insurance, cash drawers and transactions, purchasing, suppliers, materials and assets. Much of that work ends on paper or in a spreadsheet: invoices, result reports, labels and financial exports.

## What I owned

I have worked on this frontend since August 2024 and have led it since January 2026. I wrote about 70% of the commits in its current repository and most of the shared components, and I built the print, barcode and Excel workflows. Other developers have contributed screens.

## Scope

The platform has 33 screens built on a library of 57 shared components. The library includes:

- a list page and data table that every list uses
- step-by-step wizards for patients, appointments, examinations and doctors
- transaction views for each kind of party: patients, doctors, insurers, suppliers and contractors

## Key decisions

### A component library first

Most screens are lists of records with forms on top, so I kept the shared parts in one library: the list page, the table, confirmation dialogs, the wizards and the filter dialogs. A new screen is mostly configuration, and a fix in a shared part reaches every screen that uses it.

### Printing as a feature

Invoices, result reports and labels are what patients and staff actually hold, so printing is built in rather than left to the browser:

- **Invoices** put each group of examinations on its own page.
- **Result reports** print separately from the screen where results are edited.
- **Labels** cover thermal printers and disc labels.
- **Layouts** come in several versions, and the existing result layout was kept so printed reports did not change for the staff.

### Barcodes and reference ranges

Each appointment carries a barcode, which appears in its details and on its printed labels. Examinations define their reference ranges in the examination wizard. A test can have several ranges, each with a title, a gender or a comparator, and printed results show the range that applies.

### Excel where finance needs it

The patients and transactions lists export to Excel with a financial summary and a date range filter, so finance staff can work from the same filtered data they see on screen.
